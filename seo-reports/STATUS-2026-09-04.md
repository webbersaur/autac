## The two findings that matter

### 1. Hub-vs-blog cannibalisation, and the hubs were the thin page

Every commercial head term had 3-4 Autac URLs competing, and the blog guide was
beating its own hub:

| Query | Blog guide | Hub |
|---|---|---|
| "coil cords" | `/blog/coil-cable-guide/` **18.1** | `/coiled-cords/` 29.8 |
| "coiled cords" | `/blog/coil-cable-guide/` **16.5** | `/coiled-cords/` 28.9 |
| "coil cable" | `/blog/coil-cable-guide/` **15.3** | `/coil-cables/` 44.4 |

Cause was depth: `/coiled-cords/` was 1,893 words / 3 h2 against the guide's
2,990 / 10. **Internal linking was NOT the problem** - checked first, it was
already strong (155-187 editorial inbound links per hub).

### 2. The recovered traffic is almost all non-buyer

8 settled weeks (Jul 6 - Aug 30), 455 clicks / 71,626 impressions:

- **Commercial** pages: 117 clicks (26%), 6,614 impr (9%)
- **Informational** pages: 338 clicks (74%), 65,012 impr (91%)

88 of those 117 commercial clicks are the **homepage** (brand searches). Strip
home/about/shop-online and the pages that actually sell coiled cord drew
**~19 clicks in 8 weeks, about 2 a week.** The top earners are consumer-repair
topics - fixing an iron cord, untangling a phone cord, a badge reel that will
not retract - with no purchase intent for a B2B cord manufacturer.

**Do not report the impression recovery to the client as commercial progress.**
